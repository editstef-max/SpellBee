      How to play

      • Make words of 4 or more letters.
      • Every word must use the yellow centre letter.
      • Letters can be used more than once.
      • 4-letter words score 1 point. Longer words score 1 point per letter.
      • A pangram uses all 7 letters and earns 7 bonus points.
